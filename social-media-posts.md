## Posting Tips:

### Facebook (Arabic):
- Post during peak hours: 7-9 PM Egypt time
- Use engaging visuals (conference poster)
- Pin the post to the top of the page
- Encourage shares and comments
- Respond to inquiries promptly

### LinkedIn (English):
- Post during business hours: 8-10 AM or 5-7 PM Egypt time
- Tag relevant organizations and partners
- Use professional tone
- Encourage connections to share with their networks
- Engage with comments professionally

### General:
- Include high-quality conference poster image
- Add registration link prominently
- Monitor comments and respond quickly
- Create event pages on both platforms
- Share countdown posts as the date approaches
- Post testimonials from previous events (if available)
