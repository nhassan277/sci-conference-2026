**Image to Attach:** Use the awards graphic (sci2026-awards.png) - the beautiful design with the gold trophy, Islamic geometric patterns, and all award categories listed.

**Best Practices:**
1. **Use the bilingual version** for maximum reach to both Arabic and English audiences
2. **Post timing:** Morning (9-11 AM) or evening (6-8 PM) for best engagement
3. **Pin the post** to the top of your page for maximum visibility
4. **Boost the post** if budget allows to reach wider academic and research communities
5. **Tag relevant pages:**
   - Cairo University official page
   - Faculty of Science Cairo University
   - NAWAH Scientific
   - MARC (if they have a page)
   - Relevant Egyptian scientific organizations

**Engagement Strategy:**
- Respond to comments promptly
- Share in relevant Facebook groups (Egyptian researchers, PhD students, academic groups)
- Encourage participants to share with their networks
- Create an event page and link to it in comments

**Call-to-Action:**
Encourage people to:
- Click the registration link
- Share the post with colleagues and students
- Comment with questions
- Tag friends who might be interested
