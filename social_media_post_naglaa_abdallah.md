## LinkedIn Version (Professional)

**Invited Speaker Announcement | SCI 2026 Conference**

We are delighted to announce **Prof. Naglaa A. Abdallah** as an invited speaker at the Science at the Core of Innovation (SCI 2026) conference, taking place April 7-8, 2026, at Cairo University.

**About Prof. Abdallah:**

Prof. Naglaa A. Abdallah is a Professor of Genetics at the Faculty of Agriculture, Cairo University, where she previously served as coordinator of the BSc Biotechnology program (2010-2020). Her research expertise encompasses plant improvement using genome editing, plant biotechnology, food safety, and bioinformatics.

**Distinguished Career Highlights:**
• Editor-in-Chief of GM Crops & Food (Taylor & Francis)
• Named among the 100 most influential people in biotechnology
• Coordinator of the National Biotechnology Network for Expertise (NBNE)
• Member of the Institutional Biosafety Committee, Faculty of Agriculture, Cairo University
• Former Director of the International Relations Office, Faculty of Agriculture, Cairo University
• Former Director of Egypt Biotechnology Information Center (EBIC), part of ISAAA Knowledge Centre
• Published over 80 research papers in high-impact journals

**International Experience:**
• Postdoctoral Researcher: Ohio State University, The Scripps Research Institute, The Donald Danforth Research Center
• Chief of Party for USAID project establishing Center of Excellence of Agriculture
• Consultant at ACSAD for establishing Arab Biotechnology Network
• Collaborative projects with ICARDA

**Presentation Topic:**
"Novel Genome Editing Approaches for a Sustainable Future"

Prof. Abdallah will discuss the integration of genomic resources, AI optimization, and precise gene-editing tools that are transforming biotechnology. Her presentation will cover the evolution from CRISPR-Cas9 to third-generation genome editing technologies, including PASTE, retrons, epigenetic editing, and their applications in agriculture, medicine, and sustainable development.

**Conference Details:**
📅 Date: April 7-8, 2026
📍 Venue: Cairo University Conference Hall
🌐 Theme: Building Effective Partnerships between Universities and Industry

Registration is now open. Visit our website for more information.
