## About the Conference

The conference aims to **build effective bridges of collaboration between scientific research and the industrial sector**, bringing together researchers from various scientific disciplines and representatives from companies and industrial institutions under one roof to exchange ideas, expertise, and transform scientific research into practical applications that serve society and the economy.

Conference participants include:
- Graduate and postgraduate students from the Faculty of Science, Cairo University
- Faculty members and researchers from various Egyptian universities
- Company representatives and industrial sector professionals
- International and local expert speakers

## Available Partnership Opportunities

We believe that **true partnership goes beyond traditional financial support**. Therefore, we offer your esteemed company a diverse range of flexible collaboration opportunities that align with your goals and strategy:

### 1. Training Workshops

Your company can deliver a **specialized training workshop** for conference participants during the event, such as:
- How to convert research findings into viable business ideas
- Entrepreneurship skills for researchers and scientists
- Modern techniques in your field of specialization
- Good Clinical Practice (GCP)
- Any other topic aligned with your company's expertise

**Benefits for your company:**
- Direct engagement with young researchers and scientists
- Building a positive brand image as a supporter of scientific research and innovation
- Discovering outstanding scientific talents and competencies
- Company logo displayed in all conference promotional materials

### 2. Awards and Recognition Sponsorship

The conference features **seven scientific awards** with winners announced at the closing ceremony:
- Best Graduation Project
- Best Master's Thesis
- Best Doctoral Dissertation
- Best Scientific Poster
- Best Presentation
- Best Published Research
- Best Innovative Research Idea

**Your company can:**
- Sponsor one or more of these awards
- Attend research presentation sessions in your field of interest (medical, environmental, technological, etc.)
- Select the best research idea or prototype that aligns with your company's interests
- Offer support to the winning researcher (research grant, training, business incubation, employment, etc.)

**Benefits for your company:**
- Direct access to the best research ideas and innovations
- Building long-term research partnerships with distinguished researchers
- Enhancing corporate social responsibility
- Company name associated with scientific excellence and innovation

### 3. Traditional Sponsorship Packages

In addition to the innovative opportunities above, we also offer **traditional sponsorship packages** at different levels:
- **Platinum (150,000 EGP):** Includes exclusive workshop + prominent logo display + exhibition booth
- **Gold (100,000 EGP):** Logo display in promotional materials + exhibition booth
- **Silver (50,000 EGP):** Logo display on website and printed materials
- **Bronze (20,000 EGP):** Company name listed among sponsors

You can view complete details of sponsorship packages on the conference website.
